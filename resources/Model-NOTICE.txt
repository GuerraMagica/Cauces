Cauces Multi-Speaker Separation ONNX v1 (selective FP16)

This is a local integration name, not an authorship or ownership claim over the
model assets. The package processes two estimated voices and a residual using
CPU inference. Assets came from a local third-party distribution; recorded
fingerprints identify the files, not redistribution permissions.

Recorded export changes: dynamic padding, cached position tables, stable
normalization reductions, corrected high-frequency pooling edges, equivalent
GPU operations and exact-length masking; native DSP handles decoder padding.
Selected learned separator weights and 384 internal learned affine operations
use FP16 on the GPU; CPU arithmetic, normalization, attention products, length
handling and the high-frequency refiner retain higher precision. These are
descriptions recorded by the asset distribution, not claims of Cauces authorship
or independent verification of the exporter.
