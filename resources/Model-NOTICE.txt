Cauces Multi-Speaker Separation ONNX v1 (selective FP16)

This is a local integration name for existing converted ONNX assets, not a claim
that Cauces trained the weights. The package processes exactly two speakers and
a residual using CPU inference.

The source checkpoint SHA-256 recorded in the manifest matches the checkpoint of
MossFormer2_SS_16K, Alibaba Speech Lab:
https://huggingface.co/alibabasglab/MossFormer2_SS_16K
The upstream model card identifies Apache-2.0. A checkpoint fingerprint does not
prove tensor-by-tensor equivalence of the ONNX conversion or establish rights to
all converted assets. Public provenance of the refiner checkpoint is unidentified.
This personal build does not grant redistribution rights to third-party assets.
Asset fingerprints remain in Models/manifest.json and are verified before use.
